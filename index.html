🎭 The Desdemona Cloud

A Shakespearean Parody for Grades VII–IX | Screen2Skills By AANA

📊 Presentation Slides

📜 Play Script

Part 1: The Original Tragedy (Brief & Dramatic)

(The stage is dimly lit. Low, ominous, tragic orchestral music plays. Desdemona is asleep on a chair/bench. Othello paces the stage, looking tormented.)

Othello: (Gazing at Desdemona) It is the cause, it is the cause, my soul! Yet I’ll not shed her blood, nor scar that whiter skin of hers than snow…

Narrator: (Stepping out from the side, breaking the fourth wall) We all know the tragedy of the Moor of Venice. The villainous Iago convinced Othello that his wife, Desdemona, was unfaithful. The proof? A simple, lost strawberry-patterned handkerchief. No background checks. No double-checking. Just absolute chaos.

Othello: (Waking Desdemona up roughly) Have you pray’d to-night, Desdemona? That handkerchief which I so loved and gave thee, thou gav’st to Cassio!

Desdemona: (Pleading) No, by my life and soul! Send for the man and ask him!

Othello: (Overcome with rage) Down, strumpet! It is too late!

(Othello dramatically “smothers” Desdemona with a prop pillow. Desdemona freezes, comically collapsing. Othello weeps loudly. Music swells tragically.)

Part 2: The “Tech Hack” Transition

(A loud, modern “System Error / Record Scratch” SFX blares. Neon blue lights flood the stage. Desdemona immediately sits up, throws the pillow back at Othello, and looks at the audience.)

Narrator: (Blowing a whistle) Hold on! Stop the suffocating! It is the 21st century. Why are we ending marriages over misplaced laundry? Let’s hack history!

(The Tech In-Charge plays a futuristic “Smart Device Startup” sound effect.)

Narrator: What if Desdemona wasn’t just a Renaissance lady, but a tech-forward individual who tracked her belongings? Presenting… The Apple AirTag (Venice Edition).

Part 3: The Hacked Ending (Satire & Innovation)

(The scene rewinds. Desdemona resets to sleeping. Othello is pacing, but this time, he is holding a large, over-sized cardboard smartphone. Iago sneaks up behind him, rubbing his hands together villainously.)

Iago: (Whispering like a classic snake) O, beware, my lord, of jealousy; it is the green-eyed monster! Look to your wife. Did you not see her handkerchief in Cassio’s hand today?

Othello: (Gasps dramatically, clutching his heart) No! My heart is turned to stone! The strawberry handkerchief?! I am undone!

Iago: (Smirking to the audience) (Aside) My plan works perfectly. He believes me completely!

Othello: (Suddenly stops, looks at his giant phone) Wait a minute. Desdemona is terrible at keeping track of her things. She loses her keys, her crown, her mind… which is exactly why I attached an AirTag to that fabric! Let me open the Find My App.

(Tech plays a loud “Radar Beeping / Sonar Ping” sound effect that speeds up.)

Othello: (Staring at the screen, squinting) Let’s see… AirTag ‘Handkerchief’ is currently located… inside… Iago’s back pocket?!

Iago: (Eyes widen in absolute panic, freezing up) Uh… technological anomaly, my lord! A glitch in the Venetian matrix!

Othello: (Presses a button on his giant phone) Let me tap “Play Sound” to confirm.

(Tech plays a loud, unmistakable, high-pitched Chirp-Chirp-Chirp AirTag sound. Iago frantically tries to slap his own pocket to muffle the sound.)

Othello: (Marching over, pulling a giant cardboard handkerchief out of Iago’s pocket) Villain! Thou hast been pinged by local bluetooth location tracking!

Part 4: The Comedic Twist & Conclusion

(Desdemona wakes up, wearing a pair of modern sunglasses, completely unbothered.)

Desdemona: What is all this racket? Honestly, Othello, I told you I left it on the dining table, and Iago clearly swiped it while setting up the Wi-Fi router.

Iago: (Falling to his knees, pleading to the judges) Curse you, modern end-to-end encrypted location services! I would have gotten away with ruling Venice if it weren’t for you meddling kids and your ecosystem connectivity!

Othello: (To Iago) Guards! Cancel Iago’s subscription to life! Take him away to the dungeons—and block his MAC address from the palace Wi-Fi!

(Iago is comically dragged off stage by the Narrator.)

Othello: (Kneeling to Desdemona) O my fair warrior! Forgive my lack of trust. Technology hath saved me from a terrible mistake.

Desdemona: (Patting his head) It’s fine, dear. Just upgrade my cloud storage to 2TB and we’ll call it even.

Part 5: Final Bow

(The Narrator steps forward to address the judges as Othello and Desdemona strike a high-fashion editorial pose in the background.)

Narrator: And so, a tragedy that took five acts of manipulation is solved in five seconds by a piece of tech the size of a coin. Shakespeare wrote about the dangers of blind trust—but a little data transparency goes a long way.

(All actors take a fast, sharp, perfectly synchronized bow as the Tech hits a crisp, final “Success/Chime” sound effect.)

📋 Production Info

Target Grade Level

Grades VII, VIII, or IX

Play Duration

⏱️ ~6 Minutes

Cast & Roles (4 Participants Total)

  • Othello: Intense, deeply dramatic, easily misled but powerful.
  • Iago: The ultimate, smooth-talking villain (comedic & sneaky).
  • Desdemona / Narrator: Sharp, completely innocent, exasperated.
  • Tech In-Charge: Controls vital smartphone audio cues & sound effects.
